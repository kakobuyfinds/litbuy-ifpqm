What is a Litbuy spreadsheet?

03 / Keep the comparison useful

What to record beside every find.

Use these fields for a Litbuy spreadsheet product list or a personal shopping note. Mark missing information as unknown.

Comparison field What to record Why it helps
Product reference Listing link, item name, model and selected variant. Keeps similar-looking products from being mixed up.
Fit and sizing Foot or garment measurements, size chart and preferred fit. Makes sizing comparisons more useful than a letter or number alone.
Materials Listed fabric composition, upper material, lining or jewelry metal. Helps distinguish visual appearance from the stated construction.
Photo evidence Available detail photos, labels, stitching and visible condition. Supports visual checks without treating pictures as authentication.
Total budget Item cost, applicable fees, shipping estimate and import charges. Shows how the expected delivered cost differs from the item price.
Purchase terms Availability, dispatch information and return conditions. Highlights practical constraints before committing to an order.

05 / Direct answers

Before you browse.

Clear answers about this Litbuy spreadsheet guide, the category links and the limits of product research.

What is a Litbuy spreadsheet?

A Litbuy spreadsheet is a product research list associated with searches for Litbuy shopping finds. It may organize product links, categories, size notes and reference prices. A spreadsheet entry does not verify a seller, product authenticity or current availability.

Does this page provide a downloadable spreadsheet?

No. This page provides a browsing and comparison guide, with links to CAFANS product categories. It does not host a spreadsheet download or verify compatibility with Litbuy.

What should I check before choosing a product?

Check the current listing, selected variant, measurements, material description, seller terms and available product photos. Estimate item costs, service fees, shipping and any applicable import charges before ordering.

Do product photos prove authenticity?

No. Product photos can help compare visible details, but they do not establish authenticity. For branded goods, use an authorized retailer or obtain reliable authentication evidence.

Is this an official Litbuy or brand website?

No. This is an independent research and category guide linking to CAFANS. It is not an official Litbuy, Nike, Louis Vuitton, Gucci or Hermès website.
